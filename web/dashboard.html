Causal Integrity & Anti-Leakage Proof (For Academic Review)
  • Zero Lookahead: Sensor dropout is imputed using past forward-fill (limit 6h) and historical expanding medians. Future readings are never back-propagated.
  • Chronological Partitioning: No random shuffling. Test partition is strictly future chronological holdout (Nov 2026).
  • Multi-Horizon Direct Forecasting: Simultaneously predicts all 24 horizons to avoid recursive single-step error explosion.
