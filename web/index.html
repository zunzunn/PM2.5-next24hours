The Public Health Crisis

Why Retrospective AQI Fails Communities

4M+
Premature Deaths Annually

According to WHO estimates, exposure to ambient fine particulate matter (<2.5 µm) is one of the leading global environmental killers.

The Retrospective Reporting Gap

Citizens currently learn air was toxic only after inhaling it all day. Existing government monitors publish historical averages with no advance foresight.

Unmitigated Vulnerable Exposure

School children, elderly residents, and daily outdoor workers lack lead time to plan safe exposure windows, wear protection, or schedule outdoor activities.

System Architecture

How The 24-Hour Engine Works

STEP 01
Ingest

Authentic measurements ingested hourly across 647 Indian CAAQM stations from XKDR API.

STEP 02
Clean

Causal forward-fill & expanding medians. Strictly zero future lookahead or data leakage.

STEP 03
Features

Trigonometric time cycles, 8 historical lag horizons, and past 6h/24h rolling statistics.

STEP 04
Forecast

PyTorch Sequential LSTM predicts all 24 future horizons simultaneously without recursive decay.

STEP 05
Advise

CPCB category translation with automated best exposure window & peak risk alerts.
